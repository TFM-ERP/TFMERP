12  EXT. DESERT ENCAMPMENT - NIGHT12
Antara crouches by the dying embers, listening to the wind carry voices from the far tents. Across the camp a horse shifts its weight, uneasy.
ANTARA
(quietly, to himself)
Let them speak. The desert remembers every name — even mine.
A shape detaches from the shadow of the nearest tent. SHAYBOOB — lean, watchful, a knife already loose in his hand.
SHAYBOOB
(wry)
It remembers the ones who paid their debts. You still owe the night a death.
ANTARA
A death, or a name. Tonight I mean to take both.
He rises. The firelight finds the old scars across his back — a map of every master he has outlived.
SHAYBOOB
(lower)
Makhram set the price to bury you. The Asafir herd is three kingdoms deep in Persian land.
ANTARA
Then I will dig myself out three kingdoms deep.
2.
13  EXT. THE NAJD - DAWN13
First light bleeds across the dunes. ANTARA and SHAYBOOB lead two camels along a ridgeline, small against a sky the colour of beaten brass.
SHAYBOOB
We can still turn back. Abla would not call it cowardice.
ANTARA
(without looking back)
She would not have to. I would.
They crest the ridge. Below, the land opens — and far off, a thin column of dust rises where no caravan should be.
ANTARA (CONT'D)
Riders.
SHAYBOOB
(counting)
Six. Maybe eight. Dhubyan colours — and they ride like men who already know which way we run.
Antara studies the dust without fear, the way a man reads weather he has survived before.
ANTARA
Then we give them the wrong way.

3.
14  EXT. DRY WADI - DAY14
The two press into a narrow wadi, walls of cracked stone rising on either side. Hooves drum closer behind them, doubled by the echo.
SHAYBOOB
(breathless)
A dead end is still a wall at your back. I have had worse odds.
ANTARA
No. A wall at your back is a thing that cannot betray you.
Antara wheels his camel to face the mouth of the wadi. He draws his sword. The rising sun climbs the stone behind him until he is only a silhouette with an edge.
ANTARA (CONT'D)
(calling out, almost gentle)
Let them come. The desert remembers every name. After today it will remember mine.
The first rider rounds the bend — and reins up hard at the sight of one man who has decided not to run.
FADE OUT.
