WHY THIS DESIGN (from the research)

Right indicator for the wait

3–15s waits → progress + status, not a bare spinner. Skeletons/progress cut perceived wait 20–70%; an eased bar + % reads as "moving," killing the "is it stuck?" moment.

Truthful, contextual microcopy

Status reflects the real work — reading source, applying brief, drawing on the Lore Atlas, composing, gate-checking. No invented "searching the web," since the model answers from knowledge.

Branded & calming

On-brand gold sparkle, Fraunces title, the project name front-and-centre. The wait becomes a small "your story is being written" moment — and you can keep reading (non-blocking) or skip.
