WHAT CHANGES

Heavy lifting moves to Build

One run after Begin writes all 8 stages (Logline→Coverage) in order, each chained off the last. You land in Develop with a finished ladder, not a blank one.

Develop becomes a reader

No Regenerate / Gate-check / Branch. Just Advance → and ← Back through the generated stages, plus an optional “Try an alternate” when you actually want a second take.

TFM-branded, honest

The build screen uses the login's TFM identity (no star). Status is truthful — source, brief, Lore Atlas, composing — never a fake “searching the web.”
