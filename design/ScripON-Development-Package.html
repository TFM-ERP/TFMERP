Logline & Synopsis

● Livefrom Logline + Synopsis stages

A slave-born warrior-poet must cross enemy kingdoms and a cursed desert to win an impossible bride-price — and his own name.

Antara ibn Shaddad — half-Abyssinian, born property to the man who fathered him — is told he may marry Abla only if he delivers the Asafir camels, a royal herd held three kingdoms deep in Persian-controlled territory. The demand is an elegant execution. He accepts anyway, because the alternative is a life that has already been killing him slowly.

With his half-brother Shayboob he crosses the blood-enemy Dhubyan corridor, a djinn-warped no-man's-desert, and a Persian frontier at war — and is recognised, at last, by a king who respects strength over lineage. He returns with the herd and a name spoken aloud. The wedding is barely begun when a new banner crests the ridge: Aksum. The legend has only started.

Treatment

● Live≈10 beats / 3 acts · expandable

The Najd at first light — a land that looks like the beginning of the world. Antara stands outside the gate of his own people's celebration, dressed as a free man but holding borrowed ground. Makhram names his price in public; Shaddad says nothing. In that silence Antara sees the whole architecture of his non-existence — and accepts the impossible. [Read the full treatment →]
