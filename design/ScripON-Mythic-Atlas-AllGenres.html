Represent-with-care. Crime and custom palettes are authentic structural references for grounded drama, not ethnic caricature. Each carries a care tag and a short do/don’t note; the guardrail rides into the AI brief so the system writes texture, not stereotype. Sacred-rooted customs (Mahr, nikah) keep the sacred-aware lock.
WHY THIS STAYS ONE ENGINE

Same rows, new tags

Creatures and cultural palettes are the same library with different tags. A “Crime” row is just an element tagged crime + culture + care instead of fantasy + culture + sacred. Nothing new to maintain.

Anchored to setting

Pick an Arabian setting and the first crime, custom, dress and naming options are Arabian — borrow another culture from the Atlas only when you mean to.

Two-tier guardrail

Sacred-aware for living-religion roots, represent-with-care for crime & cultural custom. Both ride every pick into the brief, so authenticity never tips into caricature.
