Rain hammers the canvas. A single lamp swings overhead. NADIA (30s), a field medic with steady hands and tired eyes, presses a blood-soaked cloth to a young SOLDIER’s shoulder.

NADIA

Stay with me. Look at my eyes — not the wound. The eyes.

SOLDIER

(weakly)

…it’s so cold in here.

NADIA

Pressure — now!

The lamp steadies. For one breath, the chaos holds its breath with it.
