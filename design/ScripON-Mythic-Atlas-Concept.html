HOW THE ENGINE WORKS

One library, many lenses

A single researched element library — name · origin culture · genre tags · 1-line lore · sensitivity tier. Genre tabs and the culture anchor are just filters over it. Add Crime, Romance customs, Sci-fi later by tagging the same rows.

Anchored, not trapped

Defaults to your setting’s culture so the first options are authentic — but the Atlas lets you borrow any culture deliberately, every element keeping its origin label so blends are intentional.

Sacred-aware by design

Deities & prophets are locked out; living-religion roots get a sacred-aware tag. The guardrail rides every choice into the AI brief — respectful by default, in 12 cultures and counting.
