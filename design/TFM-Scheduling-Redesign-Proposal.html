1How film scheduling actually works

The spine is universal: the script is broken down into elements per scene; each scene becomes a strip; strips are reordered into the most efficient shooting order; that order drives the Day-Out-of-Days (cast working calendar) and every downstream document.

The optimization a 1st AD does by hand

Strips are ordered to minimise the most expensive things, in priority order: ① location (a company move can burn ~3 hours with nothing shot), ② cast availability & hold days (don't pay an actor to sit idle between an early and a late scene — that's what Drop/Pick-up is for), ③ day vs night (group so the unit isn't flipping schedules), then setup complexity. The software's job is to make that reorder fast, show the cost of each arrangement, and keep the DOOD + budget in sync automatically.

3Proposed redesign

3.1 The board, reimagined (horizontal day columns + banners + filters)

Move from vertical stacked day-cards to a true horizontal day-column board with INT/EXT colour + D/N edge stripes, banners & day-breaks (e.g. "COMPANY MOVE", "DAY OFF", "2ND UNIT"), strip locking, and a filter bar (by cast / location / D-N / set). Live warnings render inline.

3.2 Real calendar engine

Replace the naive day-ripple with a work-week aware calendar: configurable 5-day / 6-day week, weekend & holiday calendar, turnaround / rest-day handling, and day-off banners on the board — so shoot-day → date is correct and the forecast/DOOD/call-sheet dates are trustworthy.

3.3 Shooting-order optimizer (one click)

A real optimiser that reorders strips to minimise, in priority: company moves (group by location), cast hold days (cluster each actor's scenes, suggest Drop/Pick-up windows), and day/night flips — packed to a target pages/day. It shows a before/after: company moves, total cast days, idle hold days. Manual drag always overrides; locked strips stay put.

3.4 Conflict detection & gating

CheckWhat it catches
Cast double-bookingSame actor required in two units/locations on one day.
Location availabilityStrip scheduled outside the location's available window / before permit approval.
Permit windowShoot day falls outside an approved permit's validity (ties to the Clearance module).
Sun / daylight gatingEXT/Day scene scheduled outside the daylight window, or a golden-hour scene not at golden hour — using the sun engine we already built.
Over-target dayA day exceeds the pages or hours target.

3.5 DOOD upgrades

Add the Pick-up (PU) code (and Travel/Idle), DOOD versioning + dates (industry practice is to version and re-issue weekly), and PDF/CSV export of the grid for the cast department and payroll.

3.6 Multi-board scenarios (what-if)

A Stripboard/Scenario model so a 1st AD can keep Board A vs Board B (e.g. actor-availability change, a lost location), compare days/cast-days/cost, and promote one to the live board. Today there's a single implicit board.

3.7 Outputs

The classic deliverables: a one-liner (compact scene-per-line schedule), a full shooting schedule (scene detail + elements per day), the DOOD report export, and an upgraded stripboard print — each branded and PDF-ready like the call-sheet and financier pack.

4Phased roadmap (additive, reversible)

M1 · board UX
Horizontal day-column board with INT/EXT colour + D/N stripes, filters (cast/location/D-N/set), banners + day-breaks + strip lock. Pure frontend over the existing strips.
M2 · calendar engine
Work-week calendar — 5/6-day week, weekends, holiday list, turnaround, day-off banners; corrects every downstream date.
M3 · optimizer
Shooting-order optimiser (location → cast-days → D/N → pages) with before/after metrics + Drop/Pick-up suggestions; manual + locks override.
M4 · conflicts & gating
Conflict engine — cast double-book, location availability, permit window, sun/daylight gating (reuses the sun engine), over-target day. Inline board warnings.
M5 · DOOD + outputs
DOOD Pick-up code + versioning + export; one-liner + shooting-schedule + DOOD PDF/CSV; upgraded board print.
M6 · multi-board scenarios
Scenario boards (what-if A/B, compare, promote) + a reconciled script↔strip link so scene edits propagate.
