LOGLINE

A burned-out fixer has one night to move a witness across a city that wants them both dead — before the man hunting them turns out to be the only one she trusts.

SYNOPSIS

Sarah Chen runs errands no one admits to. When a routine pickup becomes a manhunt, she and Detective Riley cross a nocturnal city of brokers, bartenders and bad debts. The script's first act is taut and the dialogue crackles; momentum stalls at the midpoint, where a long stretch of Sarah-only scenes slows the engine before a strong, well-earned third act.
