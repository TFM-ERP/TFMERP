How it behaves

Clicking the TFM logo returns you to FilmOS — to the last route you came from (dip into a script from Finance → land back in Finance). “ScripON” next to it is the current-app label (not a separate link). The rail stays purely the nine workspaces — no exit item needed. On tablet/mobile the same TFM mark sits in the header/drawer and does the same job. One small ask for discoverability: keep the hover tooltip (and a focus ring for keyboard users), since you're dropping the arrow.
