How "back to FilmOS" looks — the three options

ScripON is an OS-within-the-app: FilmOS (the ERP — People, Finance, Logistics…) is the parent; ScripON's 9-workspace rail is the nested shell. The question is only how you get back out, and how you know where you are. The gold ring marks the affordance in each.

My recommendation: Option 3

“Get me out” (the rail exit) and “where am I” (the breadcrumb) are two different jobs — Option 3 does both, the way Linear/Notion/Figma handle nested contexts. If you want the absolute minimum, Option 1 alone is the safe floor (the discoverable exit is the non-negotiable part). In every case the exit returns you to the last FilmOS route, and on tablet/mobile it folds into the drawer / bottom-nav.
