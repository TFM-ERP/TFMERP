3c · Vertical micro‑drama engine MENA + global

Per‑episode Beat Engine

0:00Hook — detonate in the first 15s (the “explosion point”).
0:15Friction — a filmable conflict, not subtext.
1:00Spike — the jolt that re‑prices everything before it.
1:50Button — cut on the question, ~sec 55–58. Mandatory cliffhanger.

Generated as a timestamp skeleton before dialogue; a lint rule fails any episode that resolves.

Reversal & paywall

反转 engine: Shock (concrete evidence — a text, a transfer, a second name on a will) → Hurt (artifacts) → Release in public.

Paywall‑aware: free E1–~10 = a complete emotional argument; map is written backwards from the paywall; a major reveal lands 1–2 eps after it.

Golden three: a cliffhanger by end of E1; peaks E5 (re‑price premise), E10 (collide two secrets).
