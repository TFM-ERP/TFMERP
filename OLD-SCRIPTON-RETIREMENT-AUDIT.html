⚠ The one blocker — shared primitives live in the old file

The old ScriptOnStudio.tsx exports SxRail (the rail), SX_CSS (design tokens), cleanStageText, and the SxLadder / SxSpine / SxDirection / SxEpisode types. 14 files import them — including the new OS itself: ScriptonHome, ScriptonDevelop, ScriptonWrite, ScriptonRoom, ScriptonCompare, ScriptonVersions, ScriptonCanon, ScriptonDoctor, studio/ScriptonStudio, plus the canon/script/package/dialect pages.

Consequence: you cannot delete ScriptOnStudio.tsx until those primitives are extracted into a neutral shared module. This is step zero.

Retirement plan — ordered, low-risk

0Extract the shared primitives unblocks all

Move SxRail, SX_CSS, cleanStageText + the Sx* types out of ScriptOnStudio.tsx into scripton/shared/sx.tsx; repoint all 14 imports. Zero UX change — pure refactor. After this, the old ScriptOnStudio is just a (still-referenced) screen, not a library.

1Make the new shell responsive, delete the 26 twins

Let ScriptonShell + the new components handle tablet/mobile (they're fluid already; mostly drop the vp==='desktop' guard and add breakpoints). Then remove the vp→Tablet/Mobile branches on all 13 routes and delete the 26 *Tablet/*Mobile files.

2Drop the 'old' escape hatch, delete 7 old desktops

Remove the flag==='old' branches from the 8 dual routes + the else dashboard-chrome path in layout.tsx; retire osShellFlag's 'old' value. Delete the 7 old desktop components + the canon legacy stub. New becomes the only path.

3Decide the 5 legacy-only screens needs your call

Breakdown, Schedule, Approvals, Reports, Greenlight have no new-OS version — deleting them removes the feature. Per screen: (a) rebuild on the new shell, (b) redirect to the nearest new screen, or (c) sunset. Then delete their 15 files.

4Sweep the helpers + the flag

After their hosts are clean, delete ScriptOnRewriteSlate, ScriptOnCmdK, ScriptOnCompsDeck, ScriptOnPackagePanel, ScriptOnBuildScreen and finally ScriptOnStudio.tsx + osShellFlag.ts. No more old/new branching anywhere.
